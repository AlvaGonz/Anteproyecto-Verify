# Design System Documentation

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system, reflecting the current theme settings.

## 1. Color Palette

Our brand utilizes a vibrant and cohesive color palette to ensure a distinctive and accessible user experience. The color mode is **light**.

-   **Primary Color**: `#F98513` - This is our brand's most distinctive color, used for primary actions, key interactive elements, and prominent branding.
-   **Secondary Color**: `#223382` - A supporting color, suitable for less prominent UI elements, chips, and secondary actions.
-   **Tertiary Color**: `#9BACD8` - An additional accent color, used for highlights, badges, or decorative elements.
-   **Neutral Color**: `#DAD1C8` - This serves as our base for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements, providing a balanced and clean canvas.

## 2. Typography

Our typography system is designed for readability and hierarchy, ensuring a clear and engaging content presentation.

-   **Headline Font**: `Manrope` - Used for all major headings and titles to convey prominence and brand personality.
-   **Body Font**: `Inter` - Applied to all paragraph text, long-form content, and general UI text for optimal legibility.
-   **Label Font**: `Inter` - Used for form labels, buttons, and small textual elements, maintaining consistency with body text.

## 3. Shape and Form

The aesthetic of our UI elements is defined by their roundedness and spacing, contributing to the overall feel of the system.

-   **Roundedness**: `Subtle roundedness` (Value: 1) - UI elements feature subtle rounded corners, offering a modern yet approachable feel.
-   **Spacing**: `Normal` (Value: 2) - We employ a normal level of whitespace, providing adequate separation between elements without appearing overly sparse or dense.

## 4. Brand Guidelines

While specific named colors are not explicitly defined in this document, the core palette (`primary`, `secondary`, `tertiary`, `neutral`) guides all color applications across the system. Consistency in applying these colors, along with the defined typography and shape principles, is crucial for maintaining brand identity.
